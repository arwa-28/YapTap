# YapTap Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

YapTap is a Chrome extension that records your screen, microphone and/or system audio and saves the recording to your computer.

## What YapTap does with your data

**Nothing leaves your device.** YapTap has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

- **Screen, window or tab video**, **system or tab audio** and **microphone audio** are captured only after you click Start Recording and choose what to share in Chrome's own permission dialogs.
- The recording is processed entirely inside your browser and saved as a file to your Downloads folder when you stop.
- While you record, the data is written in small pieces to a temporary file in the extension's private storage on your own disk (so long recordings don't fill your memory). That file is deleted when you start your next recording, and any leftovers older than 6 hours are cleaned up automatically. It never leaves your device.
- YapTap does not upload, transmit, sell or share your recordings or any other data with anyone, including the developer.

## What YapTap does not do

- It does not collect personal information, browsing history or website content.
- It does not use cookies, advertising or third-party analytics.
- It does not load or run remotely hosted code.
- It does not record anything in the background. Recording only happens while you have started it, and Chrome shows its sharing indicator while it runs.

## Permissions

YapTap requests no special extension permissions. Screen capture and microphone access are granted by you, each time, through Chrome's standard prompts.

## Your recordings

Recordings are saved in your Downloads folder and are yours alone. Delete them like any other file.
